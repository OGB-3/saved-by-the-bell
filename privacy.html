Saved by the Bell

Privacy policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Saved by the Bell is a browser extension for Google Chrome and Microsoft Edge that helps you keep track of free trials and cancel them before you’re charged. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. In short: your trials stay on your computer, and we don’t collect them.

What the extension stores, and where

The extension saves the following only in your browser, on your own computer:

  • the trials you add: service name, end date and time, price, billing period, notice period, cancel link, notes and reminder times
  • what happened to each trial (active, cancelled, charged or kept) and the savings worked out from it
  • your settings: currency and default reminder time
  • which reminders have already been shown
  • whether you’ve bought the upgrade.

This information is never sent to us or to anyone else. We have no servers that receive it. Uninstalling the extension deletes it.

If you use Back up (an upgrade feature), the extension saves a copy of this information as a file on your computer, wherever your browser saves downloads. That file is yours. We never see it.

The website you’re on

When you click the extension’s icon, it checks the address of the tab you’re looking at, so it can suggest the matching service (for example, Spotify when you’re on spotify.com). This happens on your computer at that moment only. The address is not stored, recorded or sent anywhere.

The extension can’t see or change the content of web pages, and it doesn’t track your browsing.

Payments

The optional upgrade is a one-off payment handled by ExtensionPay (extensionpay.com), which uses Stripe to process cards.

  • Before you choose to upgrade, the extension doesn’t contact ExtensionPay at all.
  • When you choose to upgrade or restore a purchase, the extension asks ExtensionPay to create an anonymous key for your copy of the extension, and opens ExtensionPay’s website in a new tab. You enter your payment details on ExtensionPay’s and Stripe’s websites, not in the extension. We never see your card details.
  • After that, the extension asks ExtensionPay whether your copy has been paid for, using that anonymous key. Once it has, the extension remembers it and stops asking.
  • ExtensionPay and Stripe handle your payment information, such as your email address and card details, under their own privacy policies: extensionpay.com and stripe.com/privacy.

As the seller, we can see basic records of purchases in our ExtensionPay and Stripe accounts, such as the payment amount and date, and the email address you gave at checkout. We use these only to provide the upgrade, handle refunds and answer your questions.

Notifications

Reminders are shown using your browser’s own notifications. They’re created on your computer and aren’t sent through any service of ours.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t collect, sell or share your trials or browsing.
  • We don’t use analytics, advertising or tracking.
  • We don’t need an account or your email address to use the extension (except at checkout, if you upgrade).

This website doesn’t use cookies, analytics or tracking either.

Children

The extension isn’t directed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we’ll update the date at the top. Significant changes will be mentioned in the extension’s store listing.
