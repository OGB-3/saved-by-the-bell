How it works

  1. Add a trial in seconds. Open the extension on the service’s website and it fills in the details for you. Just add the price and when the trial ends.
  2. Get reminded in time. Before the last moment you can cancel, you get a notification. Click it to go straight to the cancel page.
  3. Cancel and celebrate. Tell the extension you’ve cancelled, and watch your money-saved total grow.
